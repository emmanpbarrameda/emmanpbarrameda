## 👨‍💻 My Story

I started coding at 16. It wasn't in school, it started with running a **Minecraft server**. I managed plugins, set up configs, and took care of a small community of players. That was the first time I built something and kept it running for real people, and it's what got me into programming.

From there, I kept learning. I did freelance projects, and those grew into real systems for real clients. Along the way I learned Java, then PHP, then Laravel and FilamentPHP, which are now my main tools.

In college, I also took on leadership roles in different school organizations. That's where I learned how to lead a team and work with different kinds of people, skills I still use today.

That experience led me to a full-time job as a Software Engineer, where I now build systems that real teams use every day. These days, I also get invited as a resource speaker at tech events, sharing what I've learned along the way with other developers.

📖 [Read my full story here →](https://emmanpbarrameda.github.io/#about-me)
